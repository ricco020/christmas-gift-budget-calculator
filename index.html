Christmas gift budget calculator: budget per person and weekly savings

Most Christmas budgets start as one number ("500 this year") and fail one person at a time: the first gifts bought get a generous share, the last people on the list get what is left. Put the total and a weight for each person (3 for a partner or child, 1 for a colleague), and the calculator gives each line its share in round amounts that add up to the total exactly. Then enter what you actually spend, add the costs that are not gifts (dinner, decorations, cards, postage), and it shows what is still to pay and how much to set aside each week until your last shopping day.

How the split by weight works

Each line gets total x its weight / the sum of all weights, in steps of the rounding you choose. Because shares are rounded down first, a few steps are left over. They go one by one to the lines that lost the most to rounding (on a tie, the line higher in the list), so the shares always add up to the total. If the total is not a multiple of the rounding step, the remainder below one step is shown as not assigned rather than hidden in someone's share. Lines with weight 0 keep the amount you typed, and the split covers what is left of the total after them.

How the weekly amount is counted

Still to pay is, for each line, planned minus spent. A line already over its plan counts as paid and is flagged in red, it does not lower the others. The money already set aside is taken off, and the rest is divided by the number of weeks (rounded up) between today and your last shopping day, rounded up to the cent. If that day has passed, the whole amount is due now. A total smaller than the sum of the plans is shown as over budget by that difference.

Limits

This is a plan, not a bank account: it does not see your card, prices or shipping cut-offs, and nothing leaves your browser (the list is kept in this browser's local storage). Currency is whatever you type.
